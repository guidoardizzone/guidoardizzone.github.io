Guido Ardizzone

PhD Candidate in Economics, Geneva Graduate Institute (IHEID)

My research is in sovereign debt, macro-finance, and banking. Using granular credit-registry and sovereign-bond data, I study how sovereign risk transmits through banks to firms, and how the seniority structure of sovereign debt shapes market access. A separate strand examines how financial sanctions reshape bank lending. I am supervised by Ugo Panizza at the Geneva Graduate Institute.
