Guido Ardizzone

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Geneva Graduate Institute (IHEID).

My research sits at the intersection of sovereign debt, macro-finance, and banking. I study how markets price sovereign risk and how that risk travels through banks to the firms they lend to. I also work on how sanctions and geopolitical risk reshape bank lending. You can find my job market paper here.
