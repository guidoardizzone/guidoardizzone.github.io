Guido Ardizzone

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Geneva Graduate Institute (IHEID), working on sovereign debt, macro-finance, and banking. I am supervised by Ugo Panizza.

I use granular credit-registry and sovereign-bond data to study how sovereign risk transmits through banks to firms, and how the seniority structure of sovereign debt shapes market access. A separate strand examines how financial sanctions reshape bank lending.
